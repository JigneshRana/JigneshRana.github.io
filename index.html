AI data intelligence for enterprise hotels

Enterprise hotel groups wanted fast answers from very large data sets. The old reporting path was slow and expensive, so we rebuilt it as an AI-based data intelligence tool.

The problem
Analytics queries took four to five minutes and the infrastructure behind them cost $2,000 a month.
What we built
An AI-based data layer designed around two hard targets, cost and speed, and shipped to enterprise hotel customers.
The result
Answers in under ten seconds and a monthly bill of $600. That saves about $16,800 a year.
Serving 40,000 customers
About 2.2 billion queries a month on provisioned Redshift plus a cache: roughly $3.9k a month in total, against $37k–$500k on pay-per-use engines. Read how.
PlanGen: requirements to code
An agentic LLM pipeline (LangChain, Pinecone, GroundX) that turns customer requirements into technical scoping, routes it through human review, and hands approved scopes to a code agent.
